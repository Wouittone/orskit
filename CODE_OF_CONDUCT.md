# Code of conduct

orskit contributors are expected to keep technical disagreement precise,
respectful, and evidence-driven. This project deals with scientific correctness
and licensing boundaries, so reviews may be direct; they should never become
personal.

## Expected behavior

- Treat contributors with respect across experience levels, backgrounds, and
  communication styles.
- Critique code, evidence, assumptions, and claims rather than people.
- Be explicit about uncertainty, missing validation, and provenance concerns.
- Make space for careful review, especially around scientific validity,
  licensing, safety, and accessibility.
- Help keep issues and pull requests focused on the concrete capability,
  defect, or decision under discussion.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Sustained disruption of technical discussions.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Pressuring contributors to bypass provenance, licensing, safety, or
  correctness requirements.
- Misrepresenting validation status, benchmark evidence, or feature parity.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate, edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict
participation when behavior undermines a safe and productive project
environment. If you need to report conduct concerns, contact a maintainer
privately when possible and include relevant links or context.
