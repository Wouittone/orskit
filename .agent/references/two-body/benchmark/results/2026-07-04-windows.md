For this specific workload on this machine, Lox measured 2.852 times Nyx's
median throughput and 3.057 times orskit's. Nyx measured 1.072 times orskit's,
and orskit measured 2.998 times Orekit's. Nyx's speed is reported with its
accuracy failure and is not accepted as evidence of equivalent propagation.
These claims do not extend to other force models, propagators, batch APIs,
operating systems, or steady-state services.
